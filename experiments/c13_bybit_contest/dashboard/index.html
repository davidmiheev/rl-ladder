Bybit contest: C8's full procedure on a year of Bybit

Every heuristic competes per contract: stage 1 screens OBI, GLFT and hfgrid over spread floor, κ and skew on 24 training sessions (one every 8 days, Aug 2025 to Feb 2026). Stage 1 numbers are in-sample (the best of 54 settings on the same 24 sessions), so they overstate; validation (Mar to Jun 2026) and the locked test (Jun to Oct 2026) decide. Stage 2 adds C8's tricks on all 48 training sessions, which include the 2025-10-10 crash; because that one session can decide the ranking, stage 2 and 3 pick under two rules, C8's (all sessions) and C10's crash-guarded one (crash drawdown at most 10%, then the best without the crash), and both picks go on to validation and the test. "C8 choice" is the configuration chosen on Binance, run unchanged on the same sessions.
